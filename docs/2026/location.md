# Location

The conference will be held at the [DoubleTree by Hilton, Edinburgh City Centre](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=DoubleTree+by+Hilton+Edinburgh+City+Centre%2C+34+Bread+Street+Edinburgh+GB).

It can be easily reached by train (Edinburgh Haymarket or Edinburgh Waverley), bus, or on foot.
If you are flying in to Edinburgh Airport you can take the tram to the _West End - Princes Street_ stop and walk approximately 10 minutes.

## Accommodation

Edinburgh has a very wide range of hotels, from budget options to luxury suites and apartments.
